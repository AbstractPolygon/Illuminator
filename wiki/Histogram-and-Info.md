# Histogram and Info

**Histogram.** The red, green and blue histogram of the picture as shown, updated as you edit.

**Info.** The camera, lens and file details of the open photo: model, capture settings, sensor size, the microlens grid and which renderer is in use (GPU or CPU). *GPU time* shows how long the GPU takes to draw a view, where the browser can measure it.

**Speed.** Under the details, a short speed report for this computer. It lists how long each stage took the last time it ran (opening the photo, measuring depth, a camera view, applying settings, animation frames, the full sensor render and its preview, clicking to focus, and so on; with the best time when a stage ran more than once), and says in plain words what limits the speed: no graphics card in use, a software renderer, a step that fell back to the processor, few processor cores or little memory, and the slowest stage so far. The times start again with each photo and fill in as you work. *Copy speed report* puts the whole report on the clipboard as text, which helps when you tell someone the app is slow.
