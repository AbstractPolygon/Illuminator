- **Faster focus changes.** Picking a new focus point shows the new picture right away, and the cleaned-up version follows a moment later. In Full sensor mode a new focus point no longer renders the whole picture again.
- **Find your photos.** Rate photos with stars, mark them as picks or rejects, add keywords, and filter the library by any of them. Other programs can read the marks too.
- **Render queue.** Line up pictures and videos to be made one after another in the background while you keep editing.
- **Easier depth work.** A depth ruler shows where things sit in depth, and you can click it to focus. Focus peaking marks the sharp edges, and Depth masks can be set by clicking the picture.
- **More ways to finish a photo.** Haze for fog and distance, tilt presets such as a miniature look, a choice of color space when saving, and H.264 video by default.
