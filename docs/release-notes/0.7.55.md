- **Film strip.** Browse a library from a strip under the picture, with ratings, picks and the ••• menu, and a Thumbnails button to go back.
- **Virtual copies and Rename.** Make another version of a photo with different settings, and rename photos, from the ••• menu.
- **Sharpening model.** An optional download that removes blur from saved photos, 3D images and video frames.
- **Choose your models.** Depth, upscaling, sharpening and fill models are optional downloads, and a window on first start shows what is available.
- **Faster and steadier.** Blur and effects draw faster, big libraries scroll more smoothly, and the tone curve and lenticular focus point work better.
