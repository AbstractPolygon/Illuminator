- **Save HDR photos.** The new *JPEG HDR* and *TIFF HDR* file formats keep the full range of light the camera captured, so bright skies, lights and reflections keep their brightness and detail: JPEG HDR shows it on HDR screens and in programs that support HDR, TIFF HDR keeps it for editing in programs such as Photoshop, Affinity, Krita and DaVinci Resolve.
