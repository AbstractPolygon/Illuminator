- **Fix blemishes and dust.** A new Retouch section above Crop has spot removal, a healing brush and red-eye correction, and a tool to select and move them.
- **Remove bigger things.** Content-aware fill paints over an object with what fits around it, Visualize spots shows where the dust is, and you can copy spots to other photos.
- **Paint your effects.** Local Adjustments has a brush mask, and ready-made brushes for soft skin, whiter teeth, a brighter iris, dodge and burn.
- **Cleaner noise.** Every mask has a Noise reduction effect that keeps edges sharp.
- **See everything while you retouch.** The picture is shown fully in focus while a retouch tool is on, and the depth of field returns when you stop.
