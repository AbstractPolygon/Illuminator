- **Click to focus lands where you click.** Clicking no longer changes the f-stop, and a thin thing you focused on stays sharp in the final picture.
- **Light L16 photos open.** Photos from the Light L16 camera (.lri) open with depth, viewpoint, focus and depth of field, with true colors from the camera's calibration.
- **Finer detail on the L16.** Zooming in sharpens what is on the screen at its full resolution, even with a crop or straighten, and Render uses every camera.
- **More tools work on every photo.** Lens correction, Reduce flare, local adjustments, retouching and 3D now work on L16 photos too.
- **New controls.** Highlight recovery brings back bright areas, vignetting has a Softness slider, and Lightroom presets import as looks.
