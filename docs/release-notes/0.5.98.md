- **Video export has a Cancel button.** Stop a video that is being recorded at any time; nothing is saved and nothing is downloaded.
- **Photos copied before a camera error are kept.** If the camera fails part way through an import, the photos already copied are added to your library and the message says which one could not be read.
- **Colors near the edges of a light field render the same on the graphics card and the processor.** A band of pixels along the border could come out wrong on the graphics card (photos are measured once more after updating).
