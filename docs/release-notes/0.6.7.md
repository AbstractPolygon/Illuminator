- **Preview your animation.** A new Preview section under Animation renders every frame, then plays it, with play and pause, frame steps, loop and a slider. Choose Full, Half, Third or Quarter size.
- **Smoother scrubbing.** The picture keeps following the time slider and playhead while a long render runs, and the playhead stays where you put it.
- **See what is rendered.** The slider is lit where frames are done, so you can watch a preview fill in.
- **One click opens a photo.** A single click on a photo in the library opens it.
