- **Copy photos from your camera over Wi-Fi.** Turn on the camera's Wi-Fi, click *+ Connect over Wi-Fi…* under the library, choose the camera's network and type its password; the photos then import as they do over a cable.
- **Works with first generation cameras, and tries the ILLUM too.** This is new and has not yet been tried with every camera: if your camera does not connect, the message says what it answered, so please send it in.
