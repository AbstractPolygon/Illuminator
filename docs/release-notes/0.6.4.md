- **Print your photos as lenticular prints.** Under Render Settings > Lenticular Print, make a print for a lens sheet that turns in 3D or plays an animation, such as a refocus, as you tilt it; preview it first, and print a pitch test to match your sheet and printer.
- **Move the viewpoint farther.** Camera > Perspective > Reach lets the scene turn up to three times as far as the camera's lens allowed, for dragging, animations, 3D, VR and lenticular prints.
- **More real movement from each photo.** With the aperture closed, the app now uses as much of the camera's lens data as each photo holds, often half again as far as before, before it has to fill in.
- **Fill in what the camera never saw.** Turn on Fill with AI (an optional download in Settings) and the background uncovered behind near objects is painted in, instead of stretched, in lenticular prints and 3D images.
- **Sharper fully in focus photos.** At f/16 and f/22 the picture is no longer softened by blending views, so everything stays crisp.
