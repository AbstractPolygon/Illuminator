- **Rate and pick right on the thumbnail.** Point at a photo to see five stars along its bottom and a pick checkbox at its top left; click to rate or pick.
- **Looks you can save and share.** Keep a photo's tone, color and local adjustments as a look, put it on other photos with a click, and send it as a small file.
- **Batch an album with a look.** In the Render Queue, Choose all, pick a look and add pictures for the whole album; your photos keep their settings.
- **Batch videos too.** Add a video for every chosen photo; photos without an animation use the open photo's.
- **A clear message when a video can't be made.** A computer that cannot encode the chosen format now says so, instead of saving an empty file.
