- **Slight blur looks smooth.** Small amounts of background blur are now even and clean instead of slightly grainy.
- **Tapping to focus works with a finger.** A tap that drifts a few pixels on a touch screen still counts as a tap.
- **Switching photos quickly is safe.** Opening another photo while one is still loading no longer mixes up edits, depth or thumbnails.
- **Replacing photos on import is safer.** If a copy fails, the original photo and its edits stay as they were.
- **Fewer broken colors and depth glitches.** White balance no longer breaks at certain temperatures, and depth along the bottom and right edges is more accurate (photos are measured once more after updating).
