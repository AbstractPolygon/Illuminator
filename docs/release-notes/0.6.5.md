- **Print Lenticular 3D images and animations.** Under Render Settings > Lenticular Print, make a print for a lens sheet that turns in 3D or plays an animation as you tilt it; preview it first, and print a pitch test to match your sheet and printer.
- **Break reality.** Camera > Perspective > Reach rotates the scene up to three times as far as the camera's lens allowed, and Fill with AI (an optional download in Settings), which tries to fill in missing photo areas.
- **Sharper fully in focus photos.** Full sensor pictures now keeps more detail, and f/16 and f/22 are no longer softened to allow fully in-focus photos.
