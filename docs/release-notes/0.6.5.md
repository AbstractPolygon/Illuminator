- **Print your photos as lenticular prints.** Under Render Settings > Lenticular Print, make a print for a lens sheet that turns in 3D or plays an animation as you tilt it; preview it first, and print a pitch test to match your sheet and printer.
- **Move the viewpoint farther.** Camera > Perspective > Reach turns the scene up to three times as far as the camera's lens allowed, and Fill with AI (an optional download in Settings) paints in what the camera never saw.
- **Sharper fully in focus photos.** Full sensor pictures now keep all the detail Lytro Desktop shows, and f/16 and f/22 are no longer softened.
- **Opens on Apple Silicon Macs.** The Mac app no longer says it is damaged; the first time, macOS may ask you to choose Open Anyway in Privacy & Security.
