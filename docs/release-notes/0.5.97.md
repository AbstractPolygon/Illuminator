- **Bright colored lights keep their brightness.** Strongly red or blue subjects, such as stage lights and LEDs, no longer come out about four times too dark.
- **A camera import cut short is no longer kept.** If the camera stops sending partway through a photo, the broken file is discarded and the message says how many photos were copied before it.
- **Photos you double-click open in Illuminator.** Opening a photo from your file manager now opens it in the app, even if Illuminator is already running.
- **Deleting never skips the trash quietly.** When a photo cannot be moved to the trash, the app asks before deleting it for good.
- **A library on a whole drive works.** Choosing a drive such as E:\ as your library no longer makes every action fail.
