                  Depth models

                  Downloaded models appear under Camera > Depth > Depth method. They see the picture as a whole and give clean object edges; the measured depth gives their scale.

                    Save look

                    A look keeps the tone, color, curves, film look and LUT. Choose what else goes in it:

                    The focus, viewpoint, crop and export settings stay with each photo. Apply a look from the list under Library; it can be undone. ••• next to a look saves it as a file to share.

                    Rating, flag and keywords

                    Rating

                    Separate keywords with commas. The marks are also saved next to the photo as an XMP file that other programs (Lightroom, Bridge, digiKam) read. Keys for the open photo: Shift+1 to 5 rate, Shift+0 clears the rating, Shift+P picks, Shift+X rejects, Shift+U clears the flag.

                        Connect to a camera over Wi-Fi

                        For first generation Lytro cameras (firmware 1.2 or later) and the ILLUM. The ILLUM over Wi-Fi is new and not yet known to work: if it does not connect, the message says what the camera answered.

                        1. Turn on the camera's Wi-Fi. First generation: swipe up on the screen to bring up the taskbar, then tap the Wi-Fi icon. ILLUM: Settings, iOS Connect, set Access Point to On; the camera shows its network name and password.
                        2. Choose the camera's network below and type its password. This computer then joins it, and is off the internet while it is joined.
                        3. Press Connect. Keep the camera on and near the computer while photos are copied.
